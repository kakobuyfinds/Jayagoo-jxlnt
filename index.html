03 / Before you buy

A short, practical checklist.

  1. 01
    Choose one category first.Separate shoes, clothing and accessories so you compare products with similar uses.
  2. 02
    Compare against something you own.Use the measurements of a well-fitting garment or suitable footwear as your reference.
  3. 03
    Read beyond the main image.Look for construction details, material descriptions, available sizes and seller terms.
  4. 04
    Estimate the complete cost.Add known shipping charges and fees, and mark any unconfirmed costs as estimates.
  5. 05
    Recheck the listing before ordering.Confirm the selected variant, current price, availability and return conditions.

05 / Clear answers

Jayagoo spreadsheet FAQ.

What this guide offers, how to use it and what to verify on the destination listing.

What does Jayagoo spreadsheet mean on this page?

This page uses “Jayagoo spreadsheet” as the reader’s search topic for organizing fashion finds. It provides a browsing guide and links to CAFANS categories; it does not provide an official Jayagoo spreadsheet or a downloadable product database.

How should I compare spreadsheet finds?

Compare the exact product variant, measurements, materials, seller information, return conditions and estimated total cost. Keep the listing link and the date you checked its details.

Does a brand name or product image prove authenticity?

No. A brand name, illustration or listing photo does not establish authenticity. Check the seller, provenance and supporting documentation, and use authorized retailers when authenticity is essential.

Where do the shopping links go?

General shopping links lead to https://www.cafans.com. Category links lead to the corresponding CAFANS category pages for shoes, clothing, headwear and accessories.

Are prices, stock or shipping costs guaranteed here?

No. This guide does not publish live prices or inventory. Confirm availability, currency, shipping charges and any applicable taxes before placing an order.
